## 4. Sections (in this order, as `##` headings, in both languages)

1. **The Lorentz force** — Circular motion in $B$: radius and period derived; velocity selector; crossed fields and drift (link to problem №8); force on a current-carrying wire derived from the Lorentz force.
2. **Sources of magnetic field** — Biot–Savart stated; field of a long wire and of a solenoid via Ampère's law (derived); field on the axis of a ring.
3. **Faraday's and Lenz's laws** — Flux; Faraday's law; Lenz's law as energy conservation; motional EMF $\mathcal E = Blv$ derived from the Lorentz force; a rod on rails: terminal velocity derived.
4. **Inductance** — Self-inductance of a solenoid derived; energy $\tfrac12LI^2$ and energy density $B^2/2\mu_0$; RL circuit current $I(t)$.
5. **LC oscillations** — Derive $\omega = 1/\sqrt{LC}$ from energy or from the circuit equation; analogy with a mass on a spring (table).
