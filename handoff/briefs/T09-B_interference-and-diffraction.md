## 3. Goal

After reading, a student can: Predict interference and diffraction patterns from path differences and phasors.

## 4. Sections (in this order, as `##` headings, in both languages)

1. **Light as a wave** — Superposition, coherence, phase and path difference $\Delta\varphi = 2\pi\Delta/\lambda$; intensity $\propto$ amplitude squared.
2. **Young's double slit** — Derive fringe positions and spacing $\Delta y = \lambda L/d$; intensity $I = 4I_0\cos^2(\pi d\sin\theta/\lambda)$ derived with phasors; effect of a thin plate over one slit.
3. **Thin films** — Phase change on reflection; derive conditions for bright/dark reflection; soap films, anti-reflection coating ($\lambda/4$); Newton's rings radius derived.
4. **Single-slit diffraction** — Derive minima $a\sin\theta = m\lambda$ by pairing zones; intensity $\text{sinc}^2$ via phasor arc; width of the central maximum.
5. **Gratings and resolution** — Grating equation; sharpness of maxima with $N$ slits (phasor polygon); resolving power $\lambda/\Delta\lambda = mN$; Rayleigh criterion $1.22\lambda/D$ (stated, with meaning).
